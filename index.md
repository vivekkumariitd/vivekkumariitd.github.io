## 🎯 What hjkl Actually Does

Most people edit text the slow way: they grab the mouse, click, drag, highlight, delete, click again. Every one of those moves costs a second. hjkl teaches the alternative — keeping your hands on the keyboard and using short, memorable key combinations to move, select, delete, and change text.

The name comes from four keys sitting next to each other on your keyboard: **h**, **j**, **k**, and **l**. In the Vim style of editing, those four keys move your cursor left, down, up, and right. Once those four clicks into place, dozens of other useful shortcuts start to make sense.

The app turns that learning into short, repeatable drills:

- A small task appears on screen.
- You perform it with the correct keystrokes.
- The app times you and compares your speed to a target.
- You repeat until beating the target feels easy.

That loop — try, measure, repeat — is why short sessions work better than long study marathons.

## 🕹️ Your First Five Minutes

When the app opens, do not try to learn everything. Here is a calm first session:

1. **Start with the four movement keys.** Practice h, j, k, and l until moving around the screen feels automatic. This is the foundation for everything else.
2. **Do one drill at a time.** Each drill is short by design. Finish one, breathe, then try it again.
3. **Look at the par time.** Do not worry if you are slower at first. Everyone is. The par is a goal, not a judgment.
4. **Read the habit comparison.** When you see the slow method written out next to the fast one, the shortcut sticks faster.
5. **Stop while it still feels good.** Ten focused minutes beats an exhausted hour.

Come back tomorrow and repeat. Short daily sessions are how these keys become muscle memory.
